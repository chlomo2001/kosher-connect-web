# Direct Debit (Bacs via Stripe) — plan for owner approval

**Status: phases are owner-gated and ship only on explicit approval.
Phase 0 verified done (Bacs already enabled on the account, owner screenshot
12 Aug). Phase 1 approved + built 12 Aug. Phases 2–3 not built.
Phase 2's eligibility rule was CORRECTED 20 Aug 2026 — read the note there
before building it; as originally written it would have collected from nobody.**

Owner ask (12 Aug): monthly SIM collection by Direct Debit, because DD survives
card replacement and costs less than cards. Decision taken with the owner:
**Stripe Bacs, not GoCardless** — we are already on Stripe rails (customers,
saved payment methods, webhook, hosted invoices); a second provider would mean
a second dashboard, webhook surface and reconciliation. GoCardless wins nothing
on price (~1% + 20p capped, both).

## Phase 0 — account prep (owner, ~10 min, no code)
1. Stripe dashboard → Settings → Payment methods → request **Bacs Direct
   Debit** on the account (UK accounts are usually approved quickly).
2. In the Bacs settings, leave **Stripe-managed mandate + pre-debit
   notification emails ON**. Bacs scheme rules require advance notice of every
   debit; Stripe sending these itself keeps us compliant with zero code and
   does not touch our own email HOLD gate.

## Phase 1 — portal mandate setup — BUILT 12 Aug (owner approved in chat)
- Portal payment-methods card grows a second option next to "Save card":
  **"Set up Direct Debit"** — sort code + account number, Stripe's Payment
  Element with `payment_method_types: ['bacs_debit']` on the existing
  SetupIntent flow. Customer sees Stripe's standard Bacs mandate language
  (Direct Debit Guarantee), which is theirs to render, not ours to write.
- Storage: **new nullable column `customers.stripe_dd_pm_id`** (migration
  file, per house rules) + `dd_mandate_status` (`pending`/`active`/
  `cancelled`). The existing `stripe_pm_id` stays cards-only — a customer can
  hold both, and nothing existing changes meaning.
- Webhook adds: `setup_intent.succeeded` routes bacs payment methods to the
  new column; `mandate.updated` / `payment_method.detached` keep
  `dd_mandate_status` honest (mandates activate ~2 business days after setup;
  we never collect against a non-`active` mandate).
- Staff view: customer card payment strip shows "🏦 DD mandate active/pending"
  next to the existing card-on-file badge.

## Phase 2 — monthly collection sweep (DRY-RUN first)

> **Corrected 20 Aug 2026.** The eligibility rule below said `paid_by = 'kc'`
> was the shop's own cost and therefore EXCLUDED, and that eligibility keyed on
> a `payment_type` column instead. Both halves were wrong, and built as written
> the sweep would have excluded every plan it exists to collect from and
> collected nothing at all. `paid_by = 'kc'` **is** through-me — the schema
> says so (`initial_schema.sql:267`, "kc fronts it vs customer pays direct")
> and `lib/mappers.js simToRow` derives one straight from the other:
> `paid_by: throughMe ? 'kc' : 'customer'`. There is no third state and no
> `payment_type` column on `sims`.

- New sweep in `pages/api/cron/sweep.js`. Eligible, all of:
  - `paid_by = 'kc'` — the shop fronts the network cost and bills it on. This
    is the typed twin of the app's `paymentType: 'through-me'`; `simToRow`
    writes both from one answer, so the column is safe to query server-side
    (the app itself reads SIMs back from `legacy_extras`, so query the column
    here and the blob there — they cannot disagree without a save bug).
  - `status` is not `cancelled` — a dead plan bills nobody.
  - the customer's `dd_mandate_status = 'active'`. A `pending` mandate is ~2
    working days from live and must never be collected against; the SIMs tab
    already distinguishes the two (`lib/simFunding.mjs`).
  - `dd_collection_day = today` (or the last day of short months).
  - the amount is greater than zero.
- Amount = the same `provider cost + max(10%, £2)` the SIM card displays today
  — one formula, no second source of truth.
  **Prerequisite:** that formula is `ddMonthlyAmount()` in `public/main.js`
  only, so a server-side sweep cannot call it. Move it to a lib module with a
  browser mirror first (the KC_MONEY / KC_SIMFUNDING pattern), or "one formula"
  becomes two the day this is built.
- Charge reference **`SIMDD-<sim uuid>-<YYYYMM>`**: unique per SIM per month.
  Ledger's unique index + Stripe's idempotency key are BOTH keyed on it, so a
  re-run, a crash mid-sweep, or a double-fired cron cannot double-charge.
- Money posting mirrors the existing card path: the sweep posts nothing; the
  `payment_intent.succeeded` webhook posts the single `payment` ledger row
  (+), matching the monthly `sim_charge` row (−) the billing already creates.
  Bacs settles in ~3 working days; until the webhook fires, the ledger simply
  shows the charge as unpaid — which is true.
- **DRY-RUN mode ships first**: the sweep builds a would-collect report as a
  keyed task ("DD dry-run 1 Sep: 14 SIMs, £322.50 — open to review") and
  charges nothing. Owner reads a month of reports before arming.
- Guardrails: per-charge cap (default £50, above it → task instead of charge);
  global daily cap (£1,000 → halt + task); collection skipped with a task if
  the mandate is not `active` or the amount is zero/negative.

## Phase 3 — failures, retries, notifications
- `payment_intent.payment_failed` (insufficient funds / refer-to-payer):
  **one retry, 5 working days later** (reference `SIMDD-…-R1` so it is also
  exactly-once). Second failure → high-priority task on the customer
  ("DD failed twice — £X for SIM …") + HOLD-gated email/SMS to the customer
  with a payment link (`createCheckoutLink`, already built).
- Late failures after success (Bacs chargeback / indemnity claim, up to
  months later): webhook `charge.dispute`/refund events post the reversing
  ledger row and open a task. Never auto-re-collect a disputed amount.
- Customer notices: Stripe sends the scheme-required pre-debit notices
  (Phase 0). Our own "payment received" receipt goes through the existing
  HOLD/TEST/LIVE email gate — nothing reaches customers until the owner has
  flipped email live, and DD works fine with it still on HOLD.
- Mandate cancelled at the bank → `mandate.updated` flips status, sweep skips,
  task opens ("DD mandate cancelled — ask customer on next visit").

## Rollout & kill switch
1. Phase 1 live → **owner sets up a mandate on their own bank account** and
   we collect £1 from it as the end-to-end proof (then refund it).
2. Phase 2 dry-run for one full cycle; owner eyeballs the report task.
3. Arm for real: env `DD_LIVE=true` (same pattern as `MAIL_LIVE`) — absent =
   dry-run forever. Unsetting it is the kill switch; in-flight charges still
   settle/refund normally via webhook.
4. Migrate customers gradually: DD is per-customer opt-in via their portal
   mandate; nobody is moved automatically.

## Explicitly out of scope
- No card-to-DD auto-switching; card-on-file flows are untouched.
- No rental/booking/repair DD — SIM monthly billing only, this pass.
- No GoCardless anything. (Their free Bank Account Data product for bank-feed
  reconciliation is a separate, unrelated backlog item.)

## Costs (for the decision)
- Stripe Bacs: ~1% per collection, min ~20p, capped (~£4) — vs ~1.5% + 20p on
  cards. A £15/mo SIM collects for ~20p instead of ~43p.
- Timing: mandate active ~2 business days after setup; each collection ~3
  working days to settle; failures can surface late — the retry/task flow
  above is the answer, not optimism.
