# Deep review — 2026-07-16 (overnight)

A kc-improve discover pass: three read-only reviewers (UX/a11y, correctness/
robustness, Phase-2 scout) over the app, then a **verify pass** — I re-read the
code for the security/High items and the doc-corrections rather than trust the
reports. "Verdict" columns say what I confirmed. Nothing was changed; this is
analysis + the groomed worklist.

The app is in good shape: real motion scale + reduced-motion umbrella, a focus-
ring layer, tabular money figures, empty states, idempotent ledger writes, an
Escape stack, a shared filter/sort control. These are the gaps in a strong base.

**Money math itself is clean** — the reviewer checked every `lib/money.mjs`
caller (`cashExpected`, `serviceOrderTotal`, `advanceOneMonth` with immutable
anchor, `ledgerSignFor`) and found no misuse; ledger writes are consistently
idempotent on a deterministic `charge_reference`.

> **None of A1-A8 should be auto-fixed by a loop** — they touch money/inventory/
> auth (judgment calls). A1 + A3 are the two worth a human fix soon: A1 wants a
> guarded/atomic decrement (a `WHERE quantity>=q` update or a DB RPC); A3 wants
> the London-day helper the sweep already uses.
